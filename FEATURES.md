# DPI All Batch Community Platform — Features

A platform built to bring our batch community together — stay connected, share memories, find opportunities, and support each other.

---

## 🔐 Account & Access

- **Sign Up & Login** — Create your account with email and password, then log in securely
- **Admin Approval** — New registrations are reviewed and approved by admins before access is granted
- **Role-Based Access** — Three levels: Administrator, Moderator, and Member — each with appropriate permissions
- **Secure Authentication** — Your password is encrypted and login sessions use secure tokens

---

## 👤 Profile & Directory

- **Personal Profile** — Add your bio, date of birth, phone number, LinkedIn, GitHub, and portfolio links
- **Skills & Expertise** — Tag your profile with the skills you have
- **Employment Status** — Share whether you're working, studying, freelancing, or open to opportunities
- **Location Info** — Let others know which city and country you're in
- **Member Search** — Find fellow batchmates by searching names or bio keywords

---

## 💬 Discussion Forum

- **Topic Categories** — Organized discussion areas (created by admins)
- **Create Posts** — Start a conversation on any topic within a category
- **Comment & Discuss** — Reply to posts and engage in conversations
- **Edit & Delete** — Modify or remove your own posts and comments
- **Moderation** — Admins can manage all content to keep discussions healthy

---

## 📢 Notice Board

- **Community Announcements** — Admins post important notices for everyone
- **Pinned Notices** — Critical information stays at the top
- **Auto-Expiry** — Notices can have expiration dates so outdated info doesn't linger

---

## 🖼️ Memory Wall

- **Share Photos & Videos** — Upload images and videos from batch gatherings and events
- **Community Gallery** — Browse through shared memories from everyone
- **Content Moderation** — Uploaders can remove their own content; admins can manage all

---

## 🎂 Birthday Dashboard

- **Today's Birthdays** — See who's celebrating today so you never miss a wish
- **Upcoming Birthdays** — Plan ahead with a list of birthdays coming up in the next week

---

## 📅 Event Management

- **Create Events** — Organize reunions, meetups, or any batch gathering with full details (date, venue, capacity)
- **Event Lifecycle** — Track events from Draft → Upcoming → Ongoing → Completed (or Cancelled)
- **Register for Events** — Sign up for events you want to attend
- **Registration Approval** — Organizers can approve or reject registrations
- **Attendance Tracking** — Mark who actually showed up
- **Waitlist Support** — Automatically handle capacity limits
- **Browse by Organizer** — See all events organized by a specific person

---

## 🗳️ Polls & Voting

- **Create Polls** — Ask the community questions with multiple choice options
- **Cast Your Vote** — One vote per person to keep it fair
- **Live Results** — See how the community is voting in real-time
- **Close Polls** — Creators or admins can close voting when ready
- **Browse Active Polls** — See all currently open polls at a glance

---

## 📚 Knowledge Sharing (Q&A)

- **Ask Questions** — Post questions and get answers from the community
- **Answer & Help** — Share your knowledge by answering others' questions
- **Upvote & Downvote** — Vote on the best questions and answers
- **Accept Best Answer** — Question authors can mark the most helpful answer
- **Sort & Filter** — Browse by newest, most voted, or unsolved questions

---

## 🤝 Mentorship

- **Register as a Mentor** — Offer guidance in your area of expertise
- **Find a Mentor** — Search for mentors by their expertise area
- **Send Connection Requests** — Reach out to mentors you'd like to connect with
- **Accept or Decline** — Mentors can review and respond to incoming requests
- **Track Connections** — See all your sent and received mentorship requests

---

## 💼 Job Board

- **Post Job Openings** — Share opportunities with the community
- **Browse Jobs** — Filter by job type and status, with paginated results
- **Update Listings** — Edit your job posts as details change
- **Status Management** — Mark jobs as Active, Closed, or Filled

---

## 🏢 Business Directory

- **Register Your Business** — Showcase your business to the community
- **Browse Businesses** — Search through the directory by name or keyword
- **Business Profiles** — View detailed information about each business
- **Manage Your Listing** — Update or remove your business information anytime

---

## 🩸 Blood Donor Registry

- **Register as a Donor** — Share your blood group and location to help others in need
- **Search for Donors** — Find available donors by blood group and location
- **Availability Toggle** — Mark yourself as available or unavailable for donation
- **Track Donations** — Update your last donation date for reference

---

## 🛡️ Admin & Moderation

- **User Approvals** — Review and approve or reject new registrations
- **Create Users** — Admins can directly create accounts with specific roles
- **Audit Trail** — Every important action (logins, registrations, approvals) is logged for accountability
- **Content Moderation** — Admins can manage posts, comments, notices, and memories across the platform
